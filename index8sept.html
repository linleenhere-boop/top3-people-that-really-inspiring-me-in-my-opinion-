The People Who Inspire Me

Tan Malaka

Tan Malaka (Ibrahim Gelar Datuk Sutan Malaka) was an Indonesian nationalist, philosopher, and revolutionary leader who is often referred to as the "Father of the Republic of Indonesia."

Sadam Permana

Sadam Permana (Sadam Permana Wiyana) is a prominent Indonesian content creator, public speaker, and legal educator. He first gained widespread public recognition when a video of his sharp and polite debate style specifically a moment where he firmly but courteously rejected an interruption went viral on TikTok.

C. S. Lewis.

Clive Staples Lewis (1898–1963), commonly known as C. S. Lewis, was a brilliant British scholar, novelist, and Christian apologist. He is widely recognized as one of the most influential intellectual figures of the 20th century, holding prestigious academic positions in English literature at both the University of Oxford and the University of Cambridge.
